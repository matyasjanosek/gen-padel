About

PADEL COMES
TO BESKYDY

Gen Padel Rožnov is a new sports project in the heart of the Beskydy mountains. Our goal is to bring modern padel infrastructure to Rožnov pod Radhoštěm.

Two outdoor courts with a pneumatic hall for year-round play, automated 24/7 access, and a premium facility. Padel for everyone — from beginners to competitive players.
